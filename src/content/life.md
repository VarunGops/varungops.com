Football has been the constant, and I captained my school, college and medical school teams. I’m a national-level skating bronze medallist, I play guitar and piano and have played in a local band, and I cook whenever I get the chance, usually trying a new way to make an old dish.

I read non-fiction and the news, I build things in Python, and I speak English, Kannada, Hindi and French, with Mandarin as the current project.

During COVID-19 I volunteered with StepOne in Bengaluru, coordinating home-isolation monitoring for more than 200,000 citizens.
