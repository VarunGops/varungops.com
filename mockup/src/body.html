About

A doctor working towards a life in surgery and the brain.

MBBS · Full GMC registration with a licence to practise · MRes Clinical Sciences, University of Liverpool

Surgery has been my goal since my pre-university years, when the rest of the class moved between the specimens and I stayed glued to the brain model, trying to work out which part did what.

I trained in Karnataka, spent a year as the first-line resident for a neurosurgical intensive care unit and its wards, and I’m now in Liverpool reading for an MRes in Clinical Sciences.

What I’ve learnt so far is that technical excellence in isolation isn’t care, and I’d like to become a surgeon whose work reaches further than his own operating list.
